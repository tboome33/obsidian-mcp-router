# Overview

Personal-mode wiki — a second-brain for the things that matter to you: people, ideas, decisions, sessions, references, and personal projects.

## Purpose
Capture life as it happens. Not a project tracker, not a research vault — a place to log conversations, file recurring concepts, and remember why you made the choices you did.

## How it's organized
- **People** — humans in your life, with relationship context and history.
- **Concepts** — frameworks and ideas you keep returning to.
- **Sessions** — daily notes and conversation logs (one file per day or per session).
- **Decisions** — choices made and the reasoning, so you can revisit them.
- **References** — external links/articles/books worth keeping with your notes about them.
- **Projects** — personal threads in progress.

## How to grow it
- Drop sources in via `wiki-ingest`.
- File the current conversation via `save`.
- Health-check periodically via `wiki-lint`.
- Read [[hot]] first when starting a session, [[catalog]] when navigating, [[journal]] to see what changed.
